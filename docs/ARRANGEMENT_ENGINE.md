# Arrangement Engine Specification

## Three Core Concepts

SongWeaver creates three distinct arrangement concepts from any input loop:

### 1. Build & Climax
- **Musical Concept**: Progressive layering and dynamic elevation.
- **Section Flow**:
  1. *Intro* (8 bars): Atmosphere & Chords only (Energy 35%)
  2. *Main A / Verse 1* (16 bars): Rhythm section enters, low-end reserved (Energy 60%)
  3. *Build* (8 bars): Snare acceleration & filter riser (Energy 78%)
  4. *Hook A / Drop* (16 bars): Full ensemble entrance, maximum groove impact (Energy 95%)
  5. *Breakdown* (8 bars): Bass & drums cut out, melodic space (Energy 40%)
  6. *Hook B / Climax* (16 bars): Maximum energy with turnaround fills (Energy 100%)
  7. *Outro* (8 bars): Deliberate subtraction toward close (Energy 30%)

### 2. Atmospheric & Deep
- **Musical Concept**: Dynamic contrast, space, and emotional resonance.
- **Section Flow**:
  1. *Ambient Intro* (8 bars): Texture pad & keys (Energy 25%)
  2. *Minimal Verse* (16 bars): Sub-bass focus without heavy drums (Energy 48%)
  3. *Tension Rise* (8 bars): Light percussive ascent (Energy 68%)
  4. *Deep Hook* (16 bars): Wide stereo panorama (Energy 86%)
  5. *Extended Breakdown* (12 bars): Melodic spotlight (Energy 32%)
  6. *Resolution Hook* (12 bars): Harmonic octave layering (Energy 88%)
  7. *Decay Outro* (8 bars): Ambient fade (Energy 20%)

### 3. Club Performance
- **Musical Concept**: High-energy DJ structure with immediate hooks and turnaround fills.
- **Section Flow**:
  1. *DJ Intro* (8 bars): Drum & bass groove tease for mix-in (Energy 65%)
  2. *Hook 1* (12 bars): Immediate main theme statement (Energy 90%)
  3. *Groove Verse* (16 bars): Rolling pocket with comping variations (Energy 70%)
  4. *Quick Build* (4 bars): Fast snare roll (Energy 85%)
  5. *Club Drop* (16 bars): Heavy bass/log-drum fills (Energy 98%)
  6. *Filter Breakdown* (8 bars): High-pass filter feel with isolated lead (Energy 55%)
  7. *Second Drop* (12 bars): Syncopated turnaround climaxes (Energy 96%)
  8. *DJ Outro* (8 bars): Clean rhythm stems for transition (Energy 50%)
